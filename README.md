# Backstage Puzzle

A four-act classroom English game for 6th graders, based on the school play story (Tara's dream).

- **Act 1 – Picture Match:** match story pictures with sentences (past continuous / past simple)
- **Act 2 – Sentence Puzzle:** join puzzle pieces to rebuild sentences from the story
- **Act 3 – Memory:** match pictures with *have* expressions (15-second preview, started by the teacher)
- **Act 4 – Quick Quiz:** 10 questions on the story, tenses and Talk Time phrases

Two-team or solo mode, sound effects and a full-screen button.

## How to play

Open `index.html` in any browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari). No installation needed.
